Pain Identification

Does Any of This Sound Familiar?

Finance teams in mission-driven organizations face predictable, solvable problems — yet most continue working around them.

📅

Reporting Takes Days or Weeks

Monthly close is a marathon sprint. Leadership is waiting. Your team is exhausted — and the data is already stale by the time it's ready.

🗂️

Data Scattered Across Systems

Spreadsheets, email attachments, accounting software that doesn't talk to anything — reconciling it all is a full-time job in itself.

📊

Forecasts Are Difficult to Maintain

A forecast built once, never updated. Or one that requires a finance PhD to maintain. Neither helps leadership make real decisions.

🏷️

Grant & Fund Tracking Is Messy

Multiple funding streams, different requirements, unclear allocation rules. Audits are stressful. Donor reporting takes forever.

⏳

More Preparing Than Analyzing

Your finance team's most valuable hours go to data wrangling, not insight generation. The work that moves the mission gets squeezed out.

✅

There's a Better Way

These aren't permanent conditions — they're symptoms of systems that haven't been modernized. The fix is structured, proven, and practical.

We spend 60–90 minutes mapping your entire finance operation — every system, every spreadsheet, every manual workaround. We score your reporting, forecasting, data structure and budgeting processes on a 1–5 maturity scale. You get a precise picture of what's costing you the most time, and why.

Process mappingMaturity scoringRoot cause analysis

Design

Based on your diagnosis, we design the right solution architecture — not a generic template.
